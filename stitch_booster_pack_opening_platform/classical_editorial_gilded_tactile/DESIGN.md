## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes classical museum curatorship with the tactile euphoria of high-stakes booster packs and fine art collecting. Tailored for discerning connoisseurs, digital art patrons, and luxury collectors, the visual universe bridges classical antiquity—fine textured vellum paper, sculpted marble bust motifs, and cardinal velvet galleries—with raw, modern artistic intervention: bold strokes of vibrant ultramarine, gilded hot-stamped foil, and tactile debossing.

The design movement is **Tactile Editorial Classicism**:
- **Curatorial Serenity:** The exploration, marketplace, and collection dashboards evoke an archive or private gallery catalogue, utilizing an ivory fine-art rag paper base (`#F7F5F0`), hairline museum borders, and literary serif proportions.
- **Nocturnal Ritual:** The booster opening sequence transitions into an immersive, deeply atmospheric cardinal velvet chamber (`#1A0B0E` / `#0D0507`), evoking private sanctums, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, and golden highlights.
- **Physicality & Metaphor:** Digital cards behave like physical relics—featuring layered matte passe-partouts, micro-beveled frames, gold leaf gilding, foil highlights, and tangible collector finishes.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture rests on museum-grade contrasts, royal pigment traditions, and an explicit hierarchy of collectible tiers:

### Core Foundation
- **Cardinal Velvet (`#6B1123`):** Primary brand accent, button states, and editorial highlights. Conveys historic authority and deep artistic passion.
- **Nocturnal Velvet Tier (`#1A0B0E` / `#0D0507`):** The ground for booster rituals, dark canvas states, and footer grounding.
- **Art Paper Ground (`#F7F5F0`):** Fine warm rag paper neutral for default gallery interfaces, sidebars, and card passe-partouts.
- **Gallery Ink (`#1C1617`):** Ultra-dark carbon black for high-contrast typographic hierarchy and delicate framing rules.
- **Ultramarine Stroke (`#0038FF`):** Vibrant, electric blue evoking pure lapis lazuli pigment; used sparingly for primary purchase CTAs, energetic strokes, and active tabs.
- **Brushed Gold & Leaf (`#D4AF37` / `#E5C158`):** Hot-stamped leaf detailing, luxury borders, collector insignia, and Legendary emblems.

### Rarity Hierarchy Tokens
- **Commune (`#8E8982`):** Stone grey / muted vellum. Clean single-line frame, matte finish.
- **Peu commune (`#2B6F55`):** Malachite deep green / antique sage. Double hairline framing.
- **Rare (`#0038FF`):** Pure ultramarine. Iridescent sheen, electric edge micro-glow.
- **Épique (`#781A88`):** Imperial amethyst / royal purple. Duo-tone chromatic foil accents.
- **Légendaire (`#D4AF37`):** Pure gold leaf. Multi-layered gilded bevels, embossed cartouche, radiant particle aura.

## Typography

Typographic expression balances classical humanist rhythm with exact digital precision:

- **Playfair Display (Display & Headlines):** Imparts museum curatorial grace, editorial prestige, and dramatic contrast. Large displays maintain generous tracking and fluid ligatures. It is used for artwork titles, gallery headers, and philosophical quotes.
- **Inter (Body & Narrative):** Provides clean, distraction-free reading for curatorial texts, descriptions, and transactional narratives, allowing the art imagery to dominate without visual clutter.
- **Space Grotesk (Labels, Technical Specs & Metadata):** Set exclusively in all-caps with generous letter-spacing (`0.1em` to `0.2em`) for serial numbering (`N° 014/100`), contract hashes, dimension values, provenance metadata, and rarity badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture mirrors architectural exhibition pavilions:

- **Grid Model:** A structural 12-column asymmetric grid for wide displays (`> 1280px`), transitioning to an 8-column layout for tablets (`768px - 1279px`), and a single or 2-column stacked flow for mobile (`< 768px`).
- **Passe-Partout Margins:** Cards and museum canvases maintain deep internal padding mimicking custom picture frames. Margins remain generous to preserve negative space, evoking spacious gallery walls.
- **Editorial Rail Layout:** Collection and Marketplace screens feature a dedicated 280px left curation filter panel, a flexible multi-card exhibition center, and an expansive right-hand Master Inspector panel for focused single-item examination.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through tactile physical layers, fine debossing, and warm atmospheric illumination rather than cold drop-shadows:

- **Surface Tiers & Passe-Partout Inset:** Surfaces feel recessed or embossed. Card artwork sits inside an inset matting bevel (`inset 0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.12), inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`).
- **Gold Leaf Reflection:** Elevated cards and rarity crests apply multi-stop foil highlights (`box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -10px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`).
- **Curatorial Hairlines:** Separation is achieved through 1px boundaries tinted in burnt umber with low opacity (`rgba(107, 17, 35, 0.12)` or `rgba(28, 22, 23, 0.1)`), removing the need for artificial shadows in light mode.
- **Nocturnal Volumetrics:** The booster opening arena utilizes radial vignettes and deep drop-shadows (`0 25px 60px -15px rgba(0,0,0,0.85)`) to focus attention on the tearing of gilded wrappers.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy honors architectural stone tablets, hand-cut museum mount boards, and parchment:

- **Geometric Restraint:** Corner radius is strictly restrained to subtle soft edges (`2px` to `4px`) for card frames, search inputs, and panels to preserve the structured editorial look.
- **Pill Exception for Ritual Controls:** Primary action buttons (`Ouvrir le booster`, filter chips) utilize elongated pill geometries (`rounded-full`) or sharp cut-corners to create dynamic contrast between structured framing and interactive triggers.
- **Chamfered & Beveled Cartouches:** Rarity labels and edition medals utilize 45-degree micro-chamfers or diamond forms, referencing classical engraved museum plates.

## Components

### Collectible Cards
- **Structure:** 3-layer architecture consisting of (1) outer embossed card base (`#F7F5F0` or dark parchment), (2) inner metallic or tonal foil border matching rarity tier, and (3) recessed artwork window.
- **Header & Footer Indices:** Top corners host edition numbers and rarity icons in `Space Grotesk`; bottom margins host Roman title in `Playfair Display` and artist name with serial edition.
- **Legendary Finish:** Features animated CSS gold leaf sheen overlay with micro-gilded border styling.

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Ultramarine Action:** Solid `#0038FF` ground with crisp white text, pill-shaped radius, accompanied by delicate inline arrow icons. Slight scale and luminous blue glow on hover.
- **Cardinal Curator Action:** Solid `#6B1123` velvet ground, subtle 1px internal gold border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`), used for direct purchases and checkout.
- **Secondary Outlined:** 1px hairline border (`#1C1617` or `#D4AF37`), transparent fill, uppercase tracked label, shifting to a soft parchment tint on interaction.

### Filter Chips & Toggles
- **Default State:** Transparent or soft bone-tinted `#EFECE4` ground, minimal border, muted text.
- **Active State:** Inverted Cardinal Velvet (`#6B1123`) or Ultramarine fill with white text and gold diamond icon indicator.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Search & Filter Inputs:** Ultra-clean flat field set on ivory paper background, bounded by a 1px border (`rgba(28, 22, 23, 0.15)`), accompanied by a classical magnifying glass icon. Focus state transitions to a refined cardinal outline.

### Booster Pack Envelope
- **Textured Foil Wrapper:** High-gloss reflective metallic packaging with crinkled foil highlights, ultramarine paint brush splatters, antique marble bust fragment visuals, and gold foil seal ready to be unsealed.
